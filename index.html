What the numbers mean

Rules & formulas

The distinction between game design and a useful numerical preview.

Three kinds of numbers

Established

Decided relationships: six attributes, weight-class modifiers, paid allocation, 10-point effective floors, height cost, weighted mental scores and bounded duration.

Tuning default

Existing design values: lineage caps and pools, age offsets and the prototype's combat references. These remain adjustable through balancing.

Preview

New illustrative values for unfinished curves, direct body HP and mixed ancestry. They are local to this planner and do not change the game specification.

Eleven weight classes

Scope

Fictional gameplay profiles describe available builds in Ironbound. Presets are examples, and equal point totals do not establish equal combat strength. Changing age changes caps, not the allocation pool. The planner refunds points when it trims a build to new limits; that is a preview convenience, not an in-game respec rule.

The skill tree retains proposed entries and unresolved gates. It does not grant profession skills, books or trained abilities to a live character. Skill effects are not added to the combat-profile totals until their curves are defined.

Source snapshot: the Ironbound design handoff, 14 October 2026, plus its existing combat prototype. All functionality runs locally; no account or connection is needed.
